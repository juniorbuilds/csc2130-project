Welcome to BizTrack

BizTrack is a Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for small and medium-sized businesses to manage stock, sales, expenditures, and profit calculations — all from a single platform that works online and offline.


Developed as part of a BSc. Computer Science project at Kibabii University, BizTrack brings enterprise-grade record keeping to any business with a smartphone or browser.


Developed by: Gerald Kipteresa  |  Registration No: COM/0122/25

Key Features

  • Stock Management — add, edit, restock, and track all inventory items with buying and selling prices
  • Sales Management — record multi-item sales with automatic stock deduction and profit calculation
  • Expenditure Tracking — log business expenses by category with a visual breakdown chart
  • Profit Calculation — instant profit-per-item and daily/weekly/monthly totals on every transaction
  • Low Stock Alerts — automatic warnings when stock drops below the minimum threshold
  • Business Reports — full Profit & Loss statement, top product analysis, and monthly trend charts
  • Offline Support — works without internet using a Service Worker and localStorage data storage
  • Installable as an App — can be installed on Android and iOS devices directly from the browser
